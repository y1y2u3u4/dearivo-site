Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Apple we receive an account identifier. We do not request your name or email.
  • Usage counter: how many messages your account sent today, to apply the daily limit.
  • Subscription status: Apple's signed transaction for Dearivo Plus (product, expiry, transaction id) to unlock Plus.

Your messages

To write a character's reply, your message and recent messages in that chat are sent to our server and passed to an AI model provider (Anthropic's Claude, accessed via OpenRouter). We do not store conversations on our server. Chats, memories and preferences are saved on your iPhone, where you can export or delete them.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps, no selling or sharing of personal data, no analytics SDKs.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Your space → Delete account. This deletes your account, usage counter and subscription link from our server and clears chats on your device. Cancel an App Store subscription separately in your Apple Account settings.

Age

Dearivo is for adults 18 and older.
